Predictive supply optimization for a consumer goods distributor

Demand algorithm tuning that reduces overstock through dynamic analysis of seasonality and regional inflation.

The Business Challenge

High inflation and fluctuating fuel costs across the Southern Cone made linear historical models fail at estimating long-shelf-life food purchases, causing inventory excess that ate into profit margin.

Our Technology Solution

We implemented a Machine Learning predictive model (time-series XGBoost) that integrates external variables like local CPI, freight prices, holidays and weather trends to generate a daily dynamic forecast per SKU and distribution center.

Results Achieved

  • Net savings of USD 1.4M per year in warehousing costs and reduced safety overstock.
  • 18% increase in the consumer division’s operating margin.
  • 99.4% availability on staple household products with no extra costs.
